What leaves this device?

When you summarize, the pasted text, selected summary options, and enabled profile/feedback guidance are sent to your selected configured AI provider through the local Node.js backend. Backend usage files contain aggregate counters, not post content. Preferences, feedback, and optional history remain in Chrome local storage.

No LinkedIn page access, cookies, automatic collection, or remote analytics are used. OpenAI and Anthropic are optional: their API keys are added only to your local server/.env.
